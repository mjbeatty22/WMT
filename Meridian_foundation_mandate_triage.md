# Meridian Foundation reserve — mandate triage

| Item | Classification | Reason |
|---|---|---|
| S1 | Hard constraint | The reserve has a seven-year horizon and must be rebalanced annually, which sets the investment period and required operating cadence. |
| S2 | Objective | The stated investment goal is to earn CPI + 4% per year, net of costs, measured over rolling five-year periods. |
| S3 | Hard constraint | The required $0.75 million quarterly payouts and one year of cash or daily-liquid government securities are explicit liquidity requirements. |
| S4 | Preference | The 60/40 benchmark provides a comparison reference, but the CIO has not said it is a required allocation or risk target. |
| S5 | Hard constraint | Long-only investing and the bans on borrowing, short sales, and derivatives are explicit portfolio restrictions. |
| S6 | Hard constraint | No single issuer may exceed 8%, an explicit concentration limit. |
| S7 | Hard constraint | Annual turnover is capped at 25% unless the committee grants approval. |
| S8 | Hard constraint | At least 90% of assets must be redeemable within five business days. |
| S9 | Unverifiable promise | A calendar-year loss limit is stated, but no return measure or breach/monitoring rule defines how compliance would be tested. |
| S10 | Missing | The pilot's optimization target has not been specified; the lack of sector, ESG, tax, and home-bias preferences does not fill that gap. |
