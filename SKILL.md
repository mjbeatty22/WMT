# SEC EDGAR Michael Burry Review

Use the shared five-year SEC panel only. Do not use estimates, market data, or facts not printed by the extractor. Apply the coded checks: operating cash flow at least net income in every displayed year; and latest-year liabilities no more than twice equity. Missing inputs are `not known`. Write a concise memo based only on printed values and end with `own the business`, `watch it`, or `pass`.

Source context: [Michael Burry's profile](https://www.michael-burry.com/michael-burry/). The fixed screening rules above—not an imitation of his writing or investment decisions—govern this exercise.
