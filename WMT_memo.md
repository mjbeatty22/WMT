# Walmart business memo

## Conclusion: own the business

Walmart's filing-based record shows an expanding, profitable business. Revenue rose from 567,762,000,000 in FY2022 to 706,413,000,000 in FY2026. Net income rose from 13,673,000,000 to 21,893,000,000 over the same displayed period.

The project owner-earnings proxy increased from 11,075,000,000 to 14,923,000,000. It remained positive in every displayed fiscal year, though it was lower in FY2025 than in FY2024. The coded ROE check was met in every displayed fiscal year. Those results support owning the business under this limited, filing-based screen.

This is a judgment about operating quality, not a conclusion about the stock's price or expected return. The memo uses no market-price data.
